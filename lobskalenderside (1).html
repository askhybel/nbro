About this calendar

This is a shared, free overview of races in and around Copenhagen – from short fun runs to half and full marathons. Anyone can submit a race, and anyone can view the list without needing to request access to anything.

Here's how it works:

  1. You submit a race through a short form (name, date, distance(s), location, and sign-up link).
  2. We check for duplicates and review the submission.
  3. The approved race shows up automatically in the public list – usually within an hour.

We don't use your information for anything beyond what's needed for the race listing itself (name, date, distance, location, and sign-up link).

Got suggestions for improvements? Send them to askhybel@gmail.com.
