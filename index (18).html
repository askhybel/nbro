About this calendar

A free, community-powered guide to running events in and around Copenhagen - from short fun runs to half and full marathons.

How to submit a race:

  1. Add details: Name, date, distance, location, and sign-up link.
  2. Quick check: Automated and/or manual review of the details to prevent duplicates.
  3. Go live: The race appears on the public list - usually within an hour.

We don't use your information for anything beyond what's needed for the race listing itself (name, date, distance, location, and sign-up link).
